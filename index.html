How we work.

I

Take the obvious route

If there's a boring way to do something, that's the way we do it.

II

Fewer parts

Every extra piece is something that can break later, so we keep cutting until only what's needed is left.

III

Mind the small stuff

Little details decide whether a tool feels right, so we spend time on them.

IV

Our own way

We're independent and opinionated. Nobody signs off on our work but us.

V

No sidecars

Companion files and helper processes count as bugs to us. If something can ship as one file, it ships as one file.

VI

Easy to maintain

We'd rather write code that's still easy to fix years from now than code that's fashionable today.

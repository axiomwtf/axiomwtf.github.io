What we believe

A short list of things we won't compromise on.

I

Simple wins

Plain beats clever. If there's an obvious way, take it.

II

Less to break

Each extra part is a future failure. Cut until only the essentials remain.

III

Details are the product

We care about the small things so the experience simply feels right.

IV

Our own way

Independent and opinionated. No committees, no padding.

V

No sidecars

Extra companion files and processes are a defect. If it can ship as one thing, it will.

VI

Built to last

We write code for the long haul. Maintenance matters more than novelty.
